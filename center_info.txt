Opening Hours:
Monday to Friday: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM
Saturday and Sunday: Closed
Closed on public holidays.

Appointment Policy:
- All appointments last 30 minutes.
- Appointments can be booked up to 30 days in advance.
- A valid email address is required before booking any appointment; a calendar
  invitation is sent to this email once the appointment is confirmed.
- A patient may have at most one confirmed appointment per doctor at a time.
  To book another slot with the same doctor, the existing appointment must
  first be rescheduled or cancelled.
- Appointments can be rescheduled or cancelled directly through this chatbot,
  free of charge, at any time before the appointment.
- Patients are asked to arrive 10 minutes before their scheduled time to
  complete any necessary paperwork.

Available Specializations:
- Dermatology
- Cardiology
- General Medicine
- Pediatrics
- Gynecology
- Ophthalmology
- Orthopedics

Location & Access:
- The clinic is located in the 3rd arrondissement of Lyon, a 5-minute walk
  from the Part-Dieu train station.
- Metro: Line B, "Part-Dieu" station.
- Free patient parking is available on-site (entrance on Rue des Lilas).
- The building is fully wheelchair accessible, with an elevator to all floors.

Other Information:
- Accepted insurance: Sécurité Sociale (French national health insurance),
  Mutuelle top-up plans, and most major private insurers.
- Payment methods: credit/debit card, cash, and contactless mobile payment.
- Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the appointment may be
  subject to a small administrative fee.
- For medical emergencies, please call 15 (SAMU) or go directly to the
  nearest emergency room — this chatbot is only for scheduling non-urgent
  appointments.
